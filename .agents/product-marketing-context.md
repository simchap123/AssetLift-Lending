# Product Marketing Context

*Last updated: May 11, 2026*

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Private lending for real estate investors -- fix & flip, DSCR rental, bridge, and construction loans in 46 states.
**What it does:** AssetLift Lending provides fast private financing for non-owner-occupied investment properties. They lend directly and also broker deals through capital partners when that's the best fit. Loan amounts range from $100K to $5M with closings possible in as fast as 5 business days.
**Product category:** Hard money lender / Private real estate lender
**Product type:** Financial services (lending)
**Business model:** Revenue from loan origination fees, interest income, and broker fees on deals placed with capital partners. No SaaS component.

## Target Audience
**Target companies:** Individual real estate investors, small to mid-size fix-and-flip operators, rental portfolio builders, real estate developers (residential)
**Decision-makers:** The investor themselves -- typically sole proprietors or LLC owners making their own financing decisions
**Primary use case:** Fast financing for investment property acquisitions and renovations when traditional bank loans are too slow or unavailable
**Jobs to be done:**
- Get funding to close on a deal before someone else does (speed)
- Finance a rehab project with minimal cash out of pocket (leverage)
- Qualify based on the property/deal, not personal income (asset-based)
- Scale a rental portfolio using DSCR loans that don't count against personal DTI

**Use cases:**
- Fix and flip: Purchase + rehab financing (up to 90% purchase, 100% rehab)
- DSCR rental: 30-year financing based on property cash flow, not personal income
- Bridge: Short-term capital to acquire or reposition before permanent financing
- Ground-up construction: Financing for new residential builds

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Real estate investors find deals faster than banks can fund them. Traditional financing is too slow, too restrictive, and doesn't underwrite based on the asset.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Banks take 30-60 days to close and require W-2 income verification
- Many hard money lenders are opaque on terms and slow to respond
- Online "fintech" lenders have rigid boxes that don't fit every deal
- Brokers add a layer of delay and confusion between investor and capital

**What it costs them:** Lost deals (another buyer closes first), carrying costs on idle capital, missed market timing, inability to scale
**Emotional tension:** Fear of losing a deal, frustration with slow/bureaucratic lenders, uncertainty about whether they'll actually close

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Other hard money / private lenders (Kiavi, Lima One, RCN Capital, Visio Lending, Fund That Flip) -- often slower response times, rigid underwriting boxes, or higher minimum experience requirements
**Secondary:** Local banks and credit unions offering investment property loans -- much slower, income-based qualification, but lower rates
**Indirect:** Using personal cash/savings, partnering with other investors, crowdfunding platforms -- limited by personal capital or diluted returns

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- Response within 24 hours (usually hours) -- faster than most competitors
- Hybrid model: lend directly AND broker through capital partners for best fit
- Work with first-time investors on a case-by-case basis (many lenders won't)
- 46-state coverage with local market knowledge (city/state landing pages)
- Full spectrum of loan products under one roof (flip, rental, bridge, construction)

**How we do it differently:** Institutional-style underwriting combined with the speed and flexibility of a private lender. Not a bank, not a broker-only shop.
**Why that's better:** Borrowers get the right capital structure for their deal, not forced into a one-size-fits-all product.
**Why customers choose us:** Speed, responsiveness, and a team that understands real estate investing from the operator's perspective.

## Objections

| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "Your rates are higher than a bank" | We trade rate for speed and certainty. A bank quote means nothing if it takes 45 days and falls through. Our borrowers close deals that bank-financed buyers lose. |
| "I've never done a deal before" | We work with first-time investors case-by-case. If the deal makes sense, we'll find a way to get it funded. |
| "How do I know you'll actually close?" | We respond within 24 hours and many files close in as fast as 5 business days. We don't string borrowers along. |
| "I already have a lender" | We're happy to provide a second quote. Many borrowers discover better terms or faster execution. |

**Anti-persona:** Owner-occupant home buyers (we only do investment properties). Borrowers looking for sub-$100K loans. People with credit scores below 660. Those unwilling to move at the speed required for private lending.

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** Current lender is too slow, lost a deal because financing fell through, tired of providing W-2s and tax returns for every loan, hit the conventional loan cap
**Pull:** Fast response times, asset-based qualification, one-stop shop for multiple loan types, responsive human communication
**Habit:** "I've always used my local bank" or "my broker handles everything" -- inertia of existing relationships
**Anxiety:** "Is this legitimate?" "Will the terms change at closing?" "Hard money sounds risky" -- trust concerns inherent in private lending
