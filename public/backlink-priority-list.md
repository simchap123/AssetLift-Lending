# AssetLift Lending - Backlink Priority List

This is the execution order for off-site links that are most likely to help crawl discovery, brand trust, and real ranking lift.

## Tier 1 - Foundational Citations

Use the existing source material in:

- `/public/directory-submission-kit.md`
- `/public/publishing-kit.md`

Submit or complete these first:

1. Google Business Profile
2. Bing Places
3. Apple Business Connect
4. Crunchbase
5. LinkedIn Company Page
6. Facebook Business Page
7. X / Twitter profile
8. YouTube channel profile
9. Linktree

Goal:

- Consistent NAP and brand data
- Branded links to the main site
- Better trust and entity recognition

## Tier 2 - Lender and Real Estate Directories

Prioritize business profiles or directory pages where lending, mortgage, or real estate investors already search:

1. Yelp business profile
2. BBB profile
3. Chamber of Commerce or local business listings
4. MerchantCircle
5. Hotfrog
6. Manta
7. Local.com
8. Alignable

Use homepage, contact, licensing, and apply links where the platform allows them.

## Tier 3 - Investor-Relevant Profile Links

Create or improve profiles on platforms where real estate investors spend time:

1. BiggerPockets company profile or founder profile references
2. Medium publication or founder article with site links
3. Blogger / Blogspot post using the publishing kit
4. Substack or newsletter profile
5. About.me or similar founder/company profile

Link targets to prioritize:

1. `/apply`
2. `/loans/fix-and-flip`
3. `/loans/dscr-rental`
4. `/tools/fix-and-flip-calculator`
5. `/tools/dscr-calculator`
6. `/markets`

## Tier 4 - Content Links That Can Actually Rank

These require outreach or publishing, not just profile creation:

1. Guest posts on real estate investor blogs
2. Guest posts on mortgage or lending blogs
3. Founder interviews on business podcasts with show-note links
4. Real estate meetup or event sponsor pages
5. Vendor/resource pages for investor communities
6. Case-study collaborations with brokers, contractors, or title companies

Best anchor themes:

- AssetLift Lending
- hard money lender for real estate investors
- fix and flip loans
- DSCR rental loans
- bridge loans for investors

Avoid repeating exact-match anchors unnaturally.

## Tier 5 - Reputation Signals

These are not classic backlinks, but they help branded search and conversion:

1. Google reviews
2. Testimonials on Google Business Profile
3. Borrower case studies on partner websites
4. Founder bios on partner or event sites

## Weekly Execution Plan

Week 1:

- Finish Google Business Profile
- Finish Bing Places
- Finish Apple Business Connect
- Finish Crunchbase

Week 2:

- Publish Linktree
- Publish Blogger post
- Publish LinkedIn Company Page
- Publish Facebook Business Page

Week 3:

- Submit 5-10 secondary directories
- Publish one founder article off-site
- Reach out to 10 investor or lending sites for guest content

Week 4+:

- Add 2-4 real backlinks per month from relevant industry sites
- Keep reviews and off-site mentions growing

## Important Constraint

These backlinks cannot be created from the repo alone. They require logging into each third-party platform and publishing the profile, article, or listing there.
